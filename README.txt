UMAR & AYESHA — PREMIUM HEART-REVEAL CINEMATIC INVITATION

OPEN
1. Keep index.html and the assets folder together.
2. Open index.html in a modern desktop or mobile browser.
3. For web hosting, upload the complete package without changing its folder structure.

FIRST-SCREEN EXPERIENCE
- The invitation opens directly on the U & A monogram, family line, names, invitation wording, and integrated heart reveal.
- The reveal is part of the first-screen composition rather than a separate widget.
- The guest traces the rose-gold heart with a mouse, finger, or stylus.
- A live gold sparkle trail follows the drawing gesture.
- Completing enough of the heart reveals the Nikah and Valimah dates.
- An accessible “Reveal the dates” fallback is included.

PREMIUM EFFECTS
1. Moving luxury silk: full-screen animated fabric folds and traveling highlights.
2. Floating golden particles: softly glowing points that drift and twinkle across the entire design.
3. Slow rose petals: translucent petals move independently across every section.
4. Gold sparkle trail: responsive star-shaped sparkles follow the heart-drawing gesture.

Additional motion includes a layered cinematic light canvas, moving lens glows, diagonal light beams, traveling silk gloss, softly moving color fields, subtle scan texture, film grain, image shimmer, scroll-linked parallax, and section reveals. Desktop uses a smooth cinematic 30 fps target; mobile uses a lighter 24 fps target, reduced pixel density, and reduced particle counts. Reduced-motion support is included.

TYPOGRAPHY
- Royal STIX: an embedded high-contrast luxury serif used for names, dates, and headings.
- Wedding Script: an embedded calligraphic accent face.
- Maison: an embedded, highly readable supporting sans-serif.
- All fonts are stored locally in assets/fonts so the design does not depend on external font services.

INVITATION FLOW
1. First-screen invitation and heart reveal
2. Countdown, unlocked after the reveal
3. Our Story
4. Celebrations
5. Venues
6. Closing

MUSIC
- Music starts on the guest’s first pointer or keyboard interaction, which complies with browser autoplay restrictions.
- The music file is assets/music/golden-dawn.wav.

CUSTOMIZATION
- Event dates and calendar details are in the CONFIG object near the bottom of index.html.
- Visible text can be edited directly in the matching HTML sections.
- Replace an image while keeping the same filename to update it without changing HTML.
